## Photography and art-direction notes

- Food and beverage assets should use dark, warm, premium restaurant lighting with natural surface reflections.
- The hero cup must be simple and unbranded: no Milo, Nestlé, Adee's, or other packaging/marks in frame.
- Avoid busy props, artificial ingredient explosions, fake steam, and wide-angle restaurant distortion.
- Supply original masters when possible; final responsive AVIF/WebP derivatives can be created during the performance phase.
- Video should leave quiet negative space for typography and be composed for both desktop and mobile crops.

## Content still required
